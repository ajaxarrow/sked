# Product

## Register

product

## Users

School registrars and administrative staff at Philippine Junior High Schools. They use this system at a desktop workstation, typically during planning periods before the school year begins. They are comfortable with spreadsheets and manual scheduling but want to eliminate the hours-long manual process of building conflict-free timetables.

## Product Purpose

Sked automates the generation of weekly class schedules for multi-section Junior High Schools (Grades 7–10). A school admin configures teachers with their subject assignments and section counts, creates sections per year level with advisers, then generates conflict-free color-coded schedules with a single click. Multiple named schedule drafts can be saved and compared per school year. The system also tracks which teachers are free at each time slot for easy substitute planning.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, efficient. The system should feel like a trusted institutional tool — not a startup product. Three words: **reliable, academic, clear**.

## Anti-references

- Generic gray DepEd / government portals (flat, low-hierarchy, dated)
- Colorful student-facing school apps (playful, gamified, inappropriate for admin work)
- Bloated Excel-like spreadsheet UIs (dense tables with no visual breathing room)
- Corporate SaaS generic feel (over-engineered, excessive chrome, feature bloat)

## Design Principles

1. **Information first** — every screen has one primary job; everything else supports it. No decorative chrome that doesn't carry data.
2. **Earned trust** — the interface signals reliability through consistent structure, predictable navigation, and clear state feedback. Admins must trust that what they see is accurate.
3. **Progressive disclosure** — show the summary; reveal details on demand. Schedule grids are complex; don't dump all controls at once.
4. **Institutional without being cold** — the maroon palette is academic authority, not bureaucratic gray. Warmth comes from careful typography and breathing room, not decorative elements.
5. **Actions are always findable** — primary actions are always visible in context, never hidden behind hover states or icon-only controls that require expertise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Color is used as emphasis, not as the sole carrier of meaning (schedule entries include subject name text, not just color). Adequate contrast for the maroon-on-white palette. Standard keyboard navigation for all forms and modals.
